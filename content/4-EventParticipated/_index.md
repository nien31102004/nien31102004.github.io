During the **First Cloud AI Journey** program, I not only completed technical lessons and hands-on AWS labs but also participated in seminars and professional sharing sessions on technology, career development, and personal skills.

These events broadened my perspective beyond configuring individual cloud services. Through the speakers' practical experiences, I learned about Hackathon preparation, real-world product development, scalable system design, data analysis, corporate culture, the DevOps career path, cloud learning strategies, teamwork, and effective work habits.

The three major events I attended were:

### [Event 1 – Seminar on Professional Knowledge and Personal Development Skills](4.1-Event1/)

This seminar covered AWS Cloud Quest, Hackathon experience, confidence building, real-world product development, and methods for reducing procrastination in study and work.

### [Event 2 – From Student to Cloud, Data, and DevOps Professional](4.2-Event2/)

This event included five presentations on career development, an AWS-based URL Shortener architecture, the role and mindset of a Data Analyst, corporate culture, and the realities of working as a DevOps Engineer.

### [Event 3 – Seminar on Professional Knowledge and Personal Development Skills](4.3-Event3/)

Held on July 25, 2026, this seminar focused on practical cloud learning, product thinking, confidence, teamwork, and task management. It also featured experiences from four Hackathon teams—Signal Scout, Plan V, 3KA, and Six Six Fuller—covering solutions in strategic signal analysis, AWS architecture automation, crowd monitoring with Computer Vision, and AI-assisted anti-money laundering.

Through these three events, I realized that a technology student needs more than technical knowledge. Communication, systems thinking, initiative, teamwork, customer-facing skills, product thinking, and the habit of documenting and sharing knowledge are also essential for long-term professional growth.
