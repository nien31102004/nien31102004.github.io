During the **First Cloud AI Journey** program, I not only completed technical lessons and AWS labs but also participated in seminars on professional knowledge, career development, and personal skills.

These events broadened my perspective beyond operating individual cloud services. Through the speakers' practical stories, I learned about Hackathon preparation, real-world product development, scalable system design, data analysis, corporate culture, the DevOps career path, and effective learning habits.

The two major events I attended were:

### [Event 1 – Seminar on Professional Knowledge and Personal Development Skills](4.1-Event1/)

This seminar covered AWS Cloud Quest, Hackathon experience, confidence building, real product development, and methods for reducing procrastination in study and work.

### [Event 2 – From Student to Cloud, Data, and DevOps Professional](4.2-Event2/)

This event included five presentations on career growth, an AWS-based URL Shortener architecture, the role of a Data Analyst, corporate culture, and the realities of working as a DevOps Engineer.

Through both events, I realized that a technology student needs more than technical knowledge. Communication, system thinking, initiative, customer-facing skills, and the habit of documenting and sharing knowledge are also essential for long-term professional growth.
